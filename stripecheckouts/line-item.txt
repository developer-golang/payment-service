LineItems is one of the most important parameters when creating a Stripe Checkout Session.

Think of it as the shopping cart that you send to Stripe.

When a customer opens the Stripe Checkout page, Stripe needs to know:

What products are being purchased?
How many?
What is the price?
What currency?

That information is provided through LineItems.
